# Scheduling rules

Hand-edited. Claude reads this on every `/reschedule` run. Prose, not config — say it the way you would say it to a person.

## Hours

Prefer 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Saturday. When a task will not fit inside that window, blocks may go outside it, but never after 11 PM.

Nothing is scheduled before 8:30 AM. The morning run is the only exception.

Sunday is off, with two exceptions: a task that is due that Sunday or already overdue gets scheduled anyway, and the weekend gym block when Saturday had no room for it. Flag it in the proposal when that happens.

## Block size

30 minutes minimum. 2 hours maximum.

## Buffers

- 15 minutes on each side of a block that sits next to a class.
- 30 minutes instead when that class is the first or the last of the day — that is travel to or from campus.
- 45 minutes after a work shift ends before the first block, for a meal. 15 minutes before a shift starts.

## Breaks

Every block is followed by a break. 5 minutes after a block shorter than 1 hour, 10 minutes after a block of 1 hour or more. Nothing goes in a break, tasks included.

Where a break and a buffer land on the same gap, the longer of the two wins — they do not add up.

## Which calendars block time

`Class`, `Work`, `Home`, `Extracurricular` and `Travel` are busy. Their events are immovable; schedule around them.

`Family`, `Fellowship Calendar`, `US Holidays` and `Birthdays` do not block time.

`Time Blocking` is the output calendar.

That is every calendar that has an event this year. `Family` and `Birthdays` each exist twice under the same title, and both copies of each are treated the same. A calendar that shows up later and is not named here is busy until it is classified — say so in the proposal rather than guessing it away.

All-day events never block time, on any calendar. Only timed events do. A birthday on `Home` or a holiday on `US Holidays` leaves the day fully schedulable.

## How long a task takes

The estimate is written by hand in the reminder's notes, in no fixed format — `2h`, `90 min`, `about an hour`. Read it as written.

A task with no estimate: assume 1 hour.

A task larger than the 2-hour maximum splits into 1-hour pieces. Those pieces may land on the same day, adjacent or not. A leftover piece under 30 minutes gets folded into another piece rather than scheduled on its own.

## Work already done, and work that ran long

A task whose blocked time has passed and is still unchecked needs more time. When its notes say how far along it is, size the next block from that. Otherwise repeat the length of the block it just used up.

A task keeps getting re-blocked this way for as long as it stays unchecked. No warning, no nag — the table already shows the blocks.

Progress notes are free-form and there is no syntax to follow. Anything that says how far along the work is, or how long it actually took, counts: `70% done`, `took 3h not 1h`, `mostly finished`. A note overrides both the original estimate and any arithmetic done against it. Most tasks will never have one, and nothing breaks when they do not.

## Order

Due date is the real signal. Overdue tasks go to the front of the queue.

Priority (EventKit's 0-9, where 0 means unset) breaks ties only.

## How much to schedule

Cap at 6 hours of task blocks per day, even when more work is pending. Tasks that do not fit under that cap are listed as unscheduled rather than crammed in. The 6 is a knob — raise or lower it after a few real weeks.

The cap counts task blocks only. The email block and the filler blocks below sit outside it, so a light task day still fills up.

## Email

Every scheduled day, Monday through Saturday, gets a 30-minute block titled `Email`.

It goes in the first free half hour at or after 8:30 AM, placed before any task block. A class, a shift or a buffer sitting there slides it later, not off the day. When a day genuinely has no free half hour inside working hours, say so under "Rules bent" rather than dropping it quietly.

## Filling the rest of the day

Tasks are the priority and go first. When every schedulable task is placed and working hours still hold unblocked time, fill that time with these, in this order, at most one hour of each per day:

1. Interview preparation
2. Reviewing course material for CS355, CS422 or MA425 — one course per block, named in the title (`Review CS355`). Pick the course with the nearest exam or due date, otherwise rotate across days.
3. Applying for jobs
4. Purdue Jiu-Jitsu practice

Same rules as any other block: 30 minutes minimum, every buffer applies, never over a busy calendar. A gap shorter than 30 minutes stays empty. These never displace a task block, never push a task past its due date, and never count against the daily cap.

## Morning run

Monday, Wednesday and Friday: one hour at 8 AM for a run and a shower. This is the one thing that goes before 8:30.

It gets blocked only when the whole 8 to 9 window is free. When it is busy there is no run that day, and nothing is moved to make room. The 10-minute break after it runs 9 to 9:10, so the first block of a run day starts at 9:10.

## Gym

One hour, Tuesday and Thursday, plus one weekend day. Saturday is preferred; Sunday only when Saturday has no free hour for it. The gym is the one activity that may land on a Sunday with no due-date reason behind it.

It is placed after task blocks and ahead of the filler ladder, in the first free hour left inside working hours. It never displaces a task block and never counts against the daily cap. When no free hour exists that day there is no gym — say so under "No time for".

## Jiu-jitsu

Practice is at a fixed hour: Monday and Wednesday 5 to 6 PM, Friday 5 to 7 PM.

It gets blocked only when the whole practice window is free and the hour after it is free too. That hour is showering — 6 to 7 PM Monday and Wednesday, 7 to 8 PM Friday — and nothing goes in it, tasks included, even though it falls outside working hours.

Friday's block is two hours because that is the practice; the one-hour-per-activity cap does not shorten it. Friday also runs past the 6:30 PM preference, which is fine — it is well inside the 11 PM bound.

When 5 PM is busy, there is no practice that day. Nothing gets moved to make room for it.

A 30-minute block sits immediately before practice, 4:30 to 5 PM, for getting ready and getting there. It goes in only when practice itself is going in and that half hour is free; when it is busy, practice still happens without it.
